🏓 Beginner's Guide to Table Tennis

📖 Basic Rules
  • Serve must bounce on your side first
  • Games are played to 11 points
  • Switch servers every 2 points
  • Straight sets win standard matches
🏓 Table Tennis Equipment
  • Rubber-faced paddle
  • 40mm celluloid or plastic ball
  • Standard 9x5 ft table
  • 6-inch high mesh net
📈 Basic Techniques
  • Shakehand and Penhold grips
  • Forehand and Backhand drives
  • Top-spin and Push cuts
  • Footwork and table positioning
